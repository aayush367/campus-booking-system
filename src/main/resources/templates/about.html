About the Booking System

Developed by University IT Services to streamline facility reservations across campus

System Overview

The University Facility Booking System was launched to address the growing demand for an efficient, centralised platform to manage campus room reservations and event scheduling.

Before this system, room bookings were handled through emails, phone calls and physical forms, leading to double bookings, scheduling conflicts and administrative overhead.

This web-based platform provides students, faculty and staff with 24/7 access to check availability, submit booking requests and receive instant confirmations.

The system covers 54 facilities across campus including lecture halls, computer labs, auditoriums, meeting rooms and specialised spaces.
